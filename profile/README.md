### A mate you can be honest with
Creators of Bruce, a virtual blue heeler pup who offers company and approachable support. Inspired by the comfort people find in their dogs, TOVA aims to make everyday support feel familiar, accessible and judgement free.

Perth-based startup developing a wellbeing app for workers to help with their mental health and provide an avenue for employers to understand and manage psychosocial risks in the workplace. Initial focus on FIFO mining workers in Western Australia, particularly those who don't feel comfortable asking for help or using conventional wellbeing apps & services.


**Features**
- Reflection: journalling, chat with Bruce and quickly check-in your mood.
- Grounding: guided breathing, quiet time with a virtual companion & other mental health management tools.
- Backed by experts: Guided conversations and tools built in partnership with mental health professionals
